# Bayt-al-Hikma

The name Bayt al-Hikma (House of Wisdom) comes from the great intellectual center in Baghdad, established under Harun al-Rashid and expanded during the Abbasid era.

It was not just a library—it was:
A centralized learning hub
A research institute
A translation and knowledge-sharing center

Scholars gathered knowledge from:
China (paper-making, mathematics)
India (astronomy, numerals)
Greece (philosophy, medicine)
Egypt (engineering, medicine)
Iberia & Persia (science, administration)

This repository follows the same philosophy, but for the modern IT world.
